“I wanted a place where nobody watches the clock but me.”Adaeze N., founder

Getting beautiful shouldn’t feel rushed.

LUSHÉ started with a simple belief: a good appointment is a calm one. Our founder, Adaeze, spent years watching clients wait in crowded salons, get squeezed between double-bookings, and leave with a style that was almost what they’d asked for.

So she built the opposite. A small studio where each client has their own time slot, their own station and the full attention of the person doing their hair, nails or makeup.

  • Your reference photo is the brief. Bring inspiration and we’ll tell you honestly what will work on you.
  • One client at a time. We plan the day so no one is waiting and no one is hurried.
  • Aftercare you can actually follow. You leave knowing how to keep the look fresh for weeks.
